# Intermediate-view prompt set

These five project assets were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The
mobile prototype's `exterior_clean_source.png` and `vehicle_front.png` were
used as binding references for the vehicle, environment, lighting, and two
endpoint compositions.

Shared prompt constraints for every angle:

- Photorealistic smartphone-video still in a 16:9 landscape composition.
- Same unbranded white five-door compact hatchback, body proportions,
  headlamps, grille, mirrors, window line, door seams, and silver multi-spoke
  wheels.
- Same concrete parking space, white bay lines, yellow-black curb, weathered
  concrete boundary, trees, apartment buildings, and soft overcast daylight.
- Chest-height level camera; whole vehicle inside the frame with small margins;
  no vertical walking bob.
- One clean frame only; no collage, UI, text, people, extra vehicles, logos,
  watermark, motion blur, vehicle movement, redesign, or horizontal mirroring.

Angle-specific requests:

1. `angle-15.png`: a shallow first step from the exact side view toward the
   front-left corner, visibly closer to the side endpoint than the 45° frame.
2. `angle-30.png`: the geometric midpoint between the approved 15° and 45°
   views, continuing the same smooth walking arc.
3. `angle-45.png`: a natural central front-left view bridging the exact side
   and front endpoints.
4. `angle-60.png`: show more of the front than the 45° view while keeping the
   same visible side; the front remains on the left half and the doors/rear
   extend to the right.
5. `angle-75.png`: a nearly head-on view that still shows a narrow amount of
   the same side as the 60° frame before reaching the exact front endpoint.

These are concept-demo frames, not a geometrically measured reconstruction.
